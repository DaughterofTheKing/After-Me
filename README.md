**Developer:** Houda Bazzi

**Project Status:** Week 2 — Initial Development

## Project Overview

After Me is an Android application designed to help individuals organize essential information, trusted contacts, and emergency instructions so loved ones know what steps to take when needed.

The goal is to provide practical, task-based guidance during emergencies or other situations when someone cannot manage their responsibilities independently.

## Business Problem

Important personal information and instructions are often scattered across documents, accounts, and devices. When an emergency occurs, trusted individuals may not know where to begin or which actions to prioritize.

After Me aims to make this process easier by organizing information around specific needs and next steps.
